# Achievement Ideas

Raw idea garden for future Wood achievements. Not all of these should ship.

## Relationship Geometry

- **Mutual Lumber**: both friends have sent exactly the same number of Woods to each other, above some threshold.
- **Perfectly Balanced**: maintain equal sent/received Woods with a friend for 7 days.
- **One-Way Street**: send 10 Woods to someone before they send one back.
- **The Long Game**: go 30 days between Woods with the same friend, then Wood them again.
- **Inner Circle**: have 3 friends where each has exchanged at least 50 Woods with you.
- **Wood Orbit**: receive Woods from 3 different friends within 10 minutes.
- **Chain Reaction**: Wood someone within 60 seconds of receiving a Wood, and they Wood someone else within 60 seconds.

## Timing Weirdness

- **At The Buzzer**: reply in the final minute before a cooldown/streak risk window expires.
- **Dawn Timber**: send your first Wood of the day before 7am.
- **After Hours**: send a Wood after midnight.
- **Lunch Break**: send Woods on 5 consecutive weekdays between 12pm and 2pm.
- **Clockwork**: Wood the same friend at roughly the same time 3 days in a row.
- **Bad Timing**: attempt to Wood someone while still on cooldown 10 times.

## Restraint And Anti-Achievements

- **Self Control**: open the app while someone is woodable and send nothing.
- **The Watcher**: view a friend's history 5 times without sending a Wood.
- **Patience, Apparently**: wait for cooldown to expire naturally instead of getting Wooded back.
- **No Notes**: have zero bug reports and 100 Woods sent.
- **Quiet Wooder**: mute someone, then still exchange Woods with them.

## Long Wood Rituals

- **Commitment Issues**: start holding a Long Wood but cancel before sending.
- **Maximum Grain**: send max-length Long Woods to 3 different friends.
- **Measured Response**: reply to a normal Wood with a Long Wood.
- **Overprepared**: hold a Wood for 9+ seconds and still get blocked by cooldown.

## Birthday And Seasonal

- **Party Plank**: send someone their Birthday Wood.
- **Many Happy Returns**: receive Birthday Woods from 3 friends in one day.
- **Festive Timing**: send a seasonal Wood in the first hour of the event.
- **Holiday Regular**: send at least one Wood in every seasonal event in a year.

## Social Oddities

- **Popular, Unfortunately**: receive Woods from 5 different friends in one day.
- **Wood Debt**: have 3 friends waiting for a reply at once.
- **Balanced Diet**: send Woods to 5 different friends in one day.
- **Favouritism**: send 80% of your Woods in a week to one friend.
- **It's Complicated**: become someone's favourite Wooder while they are not yours.

## Meta And Joke Achievements

- **QA Department**: submit a real bug report.
- **Termite Inspector**: submit a bug report that gets marked valid.
- **Super Wood?**: tap the fake purchase button.
- **Declined Transaction**: attempt to buy Super Wood and "fail" successfully.
- **Settings Enjoyer**: change your favourite wood.
- **Tasteful Grain**: set an app theme and keep it for a week.
- **Structurally Sound**: use the app for 30 days without changing settings.
